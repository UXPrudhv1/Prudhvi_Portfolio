01 / A little about me

Good design starts
with good questions.

A thoughtful process, a clear point of view, and a little curiosity go a long way.

The person behind the pixels

I’m a UI/UX Designer at Alekta Tech Solutions, working across research, wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity interfaces.

I enjoy bringing order to complexity — making products feel intuitive, accessible, and consistent without losing their personality.

My process balances user needs with product goals: understand the problem, explore the possibilities, test the details, and keep refining. Outside design, I enjoy photography, music, and discovering new perspectives.
